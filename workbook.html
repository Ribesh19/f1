POLE POSITION WORKBOOK

Hands-on workbook

From a plain report to a dashboard that tells a story.

Seven laps in Power BI Desktop. Each one ends with something you can see, and a checkpoint file in case you get stuck. Tick the steps as you go: this page remembers where you are.

TimeLaps 1 to 5: about 40 minutes. Extra laps 6 and 7: about 15 more.
You needPower BI Desktop and this folder. No sign-in, no refresh, no custom visuals.
Start withpowerbi/Starter.pbix
Written forPower BI Desktop, October 2026. Notes for older versions are marked.

Before you start

Three panes and a row of page tabs.

Open powerbi/Starter.pbix. Everything in this workbook happens on the page tabs along the bottom of the window and in the three panes on its right, plus two tabs on the ribbon.

Page tabs

The row along the bottom of the window: The story, Drivers, The race and the rest. Each one is a page of the report. Every step here starts with a label naming the page tab to be on, like this: Drivers

Visualizations

Three tabs at the top. Build visual holds the chart icons and the field wells. Format visual changes how the selected chart looks. With nothing selected, the second tab becomes Format page.

Data

The tables and fields. The ready-made measures are in the _Measures table, sorted into folders: Story, Presentation, Race and Profile.

Filters

The thin strip at the right edge of the page. Click it to open. You only need Filters on this visual.

Ribbon

Design has the theme. View opens the Selection and Bookmarks panes.
How to read a stepDark labels like Format visual are things you click. Grey labels like Finish Rate are field names or text you type. A line in grey under a step says what should happen. The small label above a step is the page tab to be on: it turns red when you need to switch page.
Buttons while editingIn Power BI Desktop, hold Ctrl and click to use a button. A plain click only selects it.

Lap 1

Two files, a whole new look.

5 MINUTES

A theme file sets colours, fonts and defaults for every visual at once. A background image carries the layout: panels, the navigation rail, the photo. You format nothing by hand.

  1. Any pageOn the ribbon, open the Design tab and click Import theme.Older versions: View tab, the small arrow at the right of the Themes gallery, then Browse for themes.
  2. Any pageChoose design/pole-position-theme.json and click Open. Close the message that says the theme was imported.The buttons, cards and charts restyle straight away. The page still looks empty on the left: that is next.
  3. The storyClick an empty part of the page so nothing is selected. In Visualizations, click the Format page tab.
  4. The storyOpen Canvas background. Next to Image click Browse... and choose design/backgrounds/main.png.Pick the .png, not the .svg with the same name.
  5. The storySet Image fit to Fit. Set Transparency to 0.The rail, the photo and the white panels appear behind the visuals.
  6. DriversGo to the Drivers page (the tabs along the bottom). Repeat steps 3 to 5 with drivers.png.
  7. The raceGo to The race page. Repeat steps 3 to 5 with race.png.
Where did the white text come from?The words on the photo and the rail were in the file all along, written in white on a white page. The decade buttons on the Drivers page became tiles because the theme says slicers look that way.
If it looks wrong
  • The background looks washed out: Transparency is not 0.
  • The picture sits small in one corner: Image fit is still Normal. Set it to Fit.
  • Nothing changed after the theme: check the file was pole-position-theme.json, then import it again.

Lap 2

Swap three charts.

8 MINUTES

Start from the question, not from the chart. Change over time is a line. A rate is not a part of a whole, so it never belongs in a pie. Work on The story page.

The table becomes a line

  1. The storyClick the table. In Build visual click the Line chart icon.You get three lines on one scale, which hides the one that matters.
  2. The storyIn the Y-axis well, remove Race Starts and Wins From Pole % with the x beside each. Keep Finish Rate.
  3. The storyFormat visual > Visual > Y-axis > Range. Type 0 in Minimum and 1 in Maximum.1 means 100%. An honest axis for a share starts at zero.
  4. The storyFormat visual > General > Title. In Text type Share of starters reaching the finish. Open Subtitle just below and in its Text type Every World Championship race, 1950 to 2025.

The pie becomes columns

  1. The storyClick the pie chart. Click the Clustered column chart icon.
  2. The storyIn the Data pane open _Measures > Story. Drag Accident Rate into the Y-axis well, under Mechanical Failure Rate.
  3. The storyThe decades are in the wrong order. On the chart click More options (...) > Sort axis > Decade. Then More options (...) > Sort axis > Sort ascending.Power BI sorts columns by size unless you say otherwise. Time must run left to right.
  4. The storyIn the Y-axis well, right-click Mechanical Failure Rate > Rename for this visual and type Mechanical. Do the same for Accident Rate: Accident.
  5. The storyTitle: What stopped them. Subtitle: Share of starts ending that way.

The donut becomes columns

  1. The storyClick the donut. Click the Clustered column chart icon. Sort it by Decade, ascending, as in step 7.
  2. The storyFormat visual > Visual: switch Data labels on.
  3. The storyTitle: The champion wins more. Subtitle: Share of a season's races.
If it looks wrong
  • The line is flat along the bottom: Race Starts is still in the Y-axis well.
  • The columns go tall to short: the chart is still sorted by value. Repeat step 7.
  • No Subtitle section: it is inside Title, below the Title text settings.

Lap 3

A headline and three numbers.

9 MINUTES

A title that names the topic makes the reader do the work. A headline states the finding. The three cards show totals nobody asked for: one filter turns each into a number with a point. Then one red column makes the same point in a chart.

  1. The storyDouble-click the title F1 Dashboard, select the words and type Formula 1 stopped breaking down.
  2. The storyDo the same to the line below it: Fewer cars fail, so the fastest car wins more often. Seventy-six seasons of evidence.
  3. The storyClick the first card (26K). Drag Finish Rate from _Measures > Story into the Fields well, then remove Race Starts with its x.The card shows 64%: the average over 76 seasons. True, and no use to anyone.
  4. The storyOpen the Filters pane. Drag Decade from the Seasons table onto Filters on this visual. Tick 1980s.The card now shows 48%.
  5. The storyIn the Fields well, right-click Finish Rate > Rename for this visual and type of starters finished, 1980s.
  6. The storySecond card (1,152): Finish Rate again, filtered to 2020s, renamed of starters finish, 2020s.88%.
  7. The storyMake that one the only red number. Format visual > Visual > Callout value > Color, and pick the red at the start of the theme colours.
  8. The storyThird card (791): Season Dominance %, filtered to 2020s, renamed of races won by the champion.55%.
  9. The storyNow make the same point in a chart. Click The champion wins more. Format visual > Visual > Columns > Color, and click the fx button beside the colour.
  10. The storyIn the window that opens set Format style to Field value. Under What field should we base this on? open _Measures > Presentation and choose Decade Colour. Click OK.Seven grey columns and one red one, the same red as the 88%.
Read it backHeadline, three numbers, three charts. Someone who looks for ten seconds now leaves with the finding. That is the test from the lecture.
If it looks wrong
  • The card still says 64%: the Decade filter went on another visual. Click the card first, then drag.
  • You see two numbers in one card: the old field is still in the Fields well.
  • The columns do not turn grey and red: the field must be Decade Colour from _Measures, not Decade from the top of the list.

Lap 4

Colour and titles that react.

5 MINUTES

Grey for context, one colour for the point. In lap 3 a measure coloured one column. Here a measure colours the leader and writes the title, so both stay right whatever the reader selects.

  1. DriversGo to Drivers and click the bar chart. Format visual > Visual > Bars > Color, and click the fx button.This is a bar chart, so the section is called Bars. On a column chart it is called Columns.
  2. DriversAs in lap 3: Format style Field value, then _Measures > Presentation > Bar Colour, OK.Only the leader stays red.
  3. DriversFormat visual > General > Title. Click the fx beside Text. Field value, Title Text, OK.The title becomes a sentence: Lewis Hamilton won 105 races. Nobody has won more.
  4. DriversIn Subtitle, type The ten drivers with the most wins.
  5. DriversClick 1990s in the row of decades.The bars, the red highlight and the sentence all change to Michael Schumacher. Click 1990s again to clear it.
What just happenedBar Colour and Title Text are ordinary measures that return text: a colour code and a sentence. Open them in the Data pane to see how short they are. They are also in guides/measures.dax.

Lap 5

Build a tooltip, then press play.

12 MINUTES

Detail belongs one step away, not on the page. First you build a tooltip from an empty page: a small report page that appears on hover. Then a play axis turns 76 seasons into twenty seconds of animation, built from four fields.

Build a tooltip page

Stuck, or short of time? The file already holds a hidden, ready-made page called Backup tooltip. Jump to step 8 and choose it as the Page.

  1. Any pageClick the green + at the end of the page tabs (its name is New page).An empty page called Page 1 opens.
  2. Page 1, the new pageClick the empty canvas. In Visualizations open Format page > Page information. In Name type Driver tooltip, then switch Allow use as tooltip on.The page shrinks to a small card. Power BI has set Canvas settings to Type Tooltip, 400 x 300, for you.
  3. Driver tooltipGo to Build visual and click Clustered column chart. Drag Season (Seasons table) into X-axis and Wins (_Measures > Core) into Y-axis.Wins in every season, all drivers together.
  4. Driver tooltipDrag the corners of the chart until it fills the small page.
  5. Driver tooltipFormat visual > Visual > Columns > Color > fx. Field value, _Measures > Presentation > Wins By Season Colour, OK.Every column stays red for now: with all drivers together, every season has a champion.
  6. Driver tooltipGeneral > Title. Click the fx beside Text. Field value, Tooltip Headline, OK. In Subtitle, type Red marks a title year.The title reads All drivers: 1,152 wins from 25,518 starts.
  7. Driver tooltipRight-click the Driver tooltip tab and hide the page.It is a part, not a page to visit. Hidden pages still work as tooltips.
  8. DriversGo to Drivers and click the bar chart. Format visual > General > Tooltips > Options. Set Type to Report page and Page to Driver tooltip.
  9. DriversHover over any bar.Your page appears, filtered to that driver: a sentence about them, their wins season by season, and red for the years they took the title.
What just happenedA tooltip page is an ordinary report page. Hovering hands it the bar's driver as a filter, so everything on it recalculates for that driver, the title included. To use it on every chart that shows drivers, open the tooltip page and drag Driver into Drag tooltip fields here at the bottom of Build visual: charts with Page left on Auto then pick it up.

The race

Short of time? Start here: the race does not need the tooltip.

  1. The raceGo to The race and click the empty chart that says Select or drag fields to populate this visual.
  2. The raceDrag four fields into its wells:
    Driver (Drivers table) into Values
    Starts To Date (_Measures > Race) into X Axis
    Wins To Date (_Measures > Race) into Y Axis
    Season (Seasons table) into Play Axis
  3. The raceFormat visual > Visual: switch Category label on.Each dot gets a name.
  4. The raceMarkers > Color > fx. Field value, Race Colour, OK.
  5. The racePress the Play button at the bottom left of the chart.Seventeen drivers race from 1950 to 2025. The red dot is whoever holds the record. Watch it change hands six times.
  6. The raceClick one dot, then press Play again.The chart draws that driver's whole path.
If it looks wrong
  • No Play button: Season is not in the Play Axis well.
  • Hundreds of dots along the bottom: you used Race Starts and Wins. The two To Date measures only show drivers with 20 or more wins.
  • The tooltip is the usual small list: Page is still Auto. Choose Driver tooltip.
  • Driver tooltip is not in the Page list: Allow use as tooltip is off. Open the tooltip page, click empty canvas, and repeat step 2. Or choose Backup tooltip for now.
  • The tooltip is cut off or has scroll bars: the chart is bigger than the page. Shrink it to fit (step 4).

Extra lap 6

Right-click to a driver's page.

5 MINUTES

Drill-through lets a reader jump from any driver on any chart to a full page about that driver. The page is already built. One field switches it on.

  1. Driver profileOpen the hidden page Driver profile from the tabs at the bottom.
  2. Driver profileClick an empty part of the page. In Build visual, scroll to Drill through at the bottom.
  3. Driver profileDrag Driver from the Drivers table onto Add drill-through fields here.Power BI adds its own back arrow in the top-left corner. Delete it: the page already has a Back button.
  4. DriversGo to Drivers. Right-click any bar > Drill through > Driver profile.The page opens for that driver only.
  5. Driver profileHold Ctrl and click Back.
It works everywhereTry it from a dot on The race, or from the scatter chart on Drivers. Any visual that shows a driver now offers the same jump.

Extra lap 7

A note that opens and closes.

8 MINUTES

A bookmark saves which things on a page are shown and which are hidden. Two bookmarks and two buttons make a note the reader can open when they want the small print. Work on The story.

  1. The storyOn the ribbon open View and switch on Selection and Bookmarks.Selection lists everything on the page. Three items at the top are hidden: Close button, Open button and Reading note.
  2. The storyIn Selection, click the eye beside Reading note and beside Close button to show them. Leave Open button hidden.
  3. The storyIn Bookmarks click Add. Double-click the new bookmark and name it Note open. Click its ... and untick Data.Without Data, the bookmark changes what is visible and leaves filters alone.
  4. The storyBack in Selection: hide Reading note and Close button, show Open button.
  5. The storyAdd a second bookmark, name it Note closed, untick Data.
  6. The storyClick the How to read this button on the page. In the format pane open Action and switch it on. Type: Bookmark. Bookmark: Note open.
  7. The storyClick the Note open bookmark so the other button shows. Click Close the note and give it the action Bookmark > Note closed.
  8. The storyHold Ctrl and click each button to try it.
See one that is already builtThe Chart Clinic page uses the same trick for its Show the verdicts button.

What next

Change the story.

You now have a theme, a layout and five techniques that work on any data. Three ways to keep going:

Ask a new question

Which decade was most dangerous? Which driver gained the most places? Build a page for it on race.png or drivers.png.

Draw your own layout

Open design/Backgrounds.pptx, move the shapes, export the slide as PNG. Steps are in guides/backgrounds.md.

Use your own data

Import the theme into your coursework report first. It is the fastest improvement you can make.
